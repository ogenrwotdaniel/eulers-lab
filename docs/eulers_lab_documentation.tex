\begin{wpbox}[wpnavy]{Executive Summary}
Most productivity tools fail at the exact moment willpower is lowest: at \textbf{03:00} when a sleepy thumb hits \emph{Snooze}, mid-lecture when a 25-minute timer offers a one-tap \emph{Stop} button, or at night when ``just five minutes'' of short-form video becomes two hours.

\textbf{Euler's Lab} (formerly \emph{WakeProof}, renamed after the mathematician Leonhard Euler, 1707--1783) is a personal, sideloaded study and research instrument. It removes the negotiation across the whole day while staying \textbf{private and offline-first}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Proof-of-Waking Alarms:} Alarms carry no dismiss button. Ringing stops only after on-device ML~Kit pose detection counts your push-ups, you answer your due flashcards, you solve an unbroken run of mental arithmetic, or (in the evening) you write tomorrow's top three.
  \item \textbf{Nuclear Deep-Work Sessions:} Fixed sessions (1\,h, 1\,h\,30, 2\,h, 3\,h, or the exact length of a calendar event) have \textbf{no pause and no stop button}, walling non-study apps on phone, tablet and watch at once --- breaks included --- and end with a mandatory handwritten session summary.
  \item \textbf{A Researcher's Study Suite:} SM-2 flashcards with optional Gemini drafting of proof skeletons and counterexample audits, a Socratic ``Euler referee'' chat, an open-question ledger, recall notes, exam-driven targets, seamless ambient loops or your own music, and an on-device weekly report.
  \item \textbf{Fixed Distraction Walls \& Minimal Launcher:} Social, video, messaging, portal and browser apps open \textbf{only 12:25--12:50 every day}, behind walls fixed in code, notification and media guards, a night wall (\textbf{18:00--08:00} on the phone, \textbf{20:00--03:00} on the tablet), exam-week shutdowns and a calm text-only home screen.
\end{enumerate}
\end{wpbox}

The Android application ID stays \texttt{com.ogenrwotdaniel.wakeproof}, so installs, sync and watch pairing survived the rename.

\subsection{One Ringing Device, One Shared Session}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ringing role (\texttt{AlarmDevicePolicy}):} Alarm rows sync so every screen shows the same schedule, but \emph{ringing} is a local, device-protected setting: on by default on a telephony phone, off on a tablet (which then cancels every local \texttt{AlarmManager} intent and shows a companion banner).
  \item \textbf{One session, every device (\texttt{SharedSession}):} A focus session started anywhere is published whole (phase, phase end, plan). Other devices offer \textbf{Continue here}, which runs the same countdown; a block finished on two devices is logged once. Every device posts a short heads-up when another device's session starts, changes phase or finishes.
\end{itemize}

\needspace{10\baselineskip}
\section{Mission-Verified Alarms}

\subsection{High-Reliability Alarm Engine}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exact delivery:} \texttt{AlarmManager.setAlarmClock()} (Doze-immune, shown in the status bar), a full-screen lock-screen alarm, and a foreground ringing service.
  \item \textbf{Direct-Boot mirror:} Every armed alarm is copied into device-protected storage (\texttt{DirectBoot\allowbreak AlarmStore}), so an alarm rings on time even when the phone rebooted overnight and was never unlocked.
  \item \textbf{Weekly repeat masks:} A 7-bit mask (Monday = bit 0 \dots Sunday = bit 6); an empty mask means every day, so an alarm can never silently disarm itself.
  \item \textbf{No ghost alarms:} \texttt{AlarmInitializer} diffs Room rows (tombstones included) against the Direct-Boot store at every start and sync, arming new alarms before cancelling orphans (daily, snooze and test intents together). At fire time the row is checked again; if the database cannot be read, the alarm rings anyway.
  \item \textbf{Own alarm sound:} Every alarm plays a personal bundled sound when present and falls back to the system alarm otherwise. All times honour the device's 12/24-hour setting.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Verification Missions}

\subsubsection[1. Push-Ups (100\% On-Device ML Kit)]{1. Push-Ups \texorpdfstring{\pill{100\% On-Device ML Kit}}{(100\% On-Device ML Kit)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item CameraX (front or rear, flippable live) feeds ML~Kit Pose Detection; a pure state machine (\texttt{PushupCounter}) follows the shoulder--elbow--wrist angle through each rep and shows a live skeleton and counter.
  \item \textbf{5 to 10 reps} across the whole app. After rep~1 the ringtone steps down from $60\%$ to $15\%$, and comes back to full volume if you stop.
  \item Frames are analysed in memory and discarded: no photo or video is ever saved or sent.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[2. Flashcard Review (SM-2, Camera-Free)]{2. Flashcard Review \texorpdfstring{\pill{SM-2, Camera-Free}}{(SM-2, Camera-Free)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Up to 10 cards due today across every subject, graded \emph{Again / Hard / Good / Easy} exactly as on the Flashcards screen. The answer can be shown only after the question has stood for three seconds; a card graded \emph{Again} comes round again and does not count.
  \item In the two weeks before an exam the alarm asks for 15 or 20 cards, the exam's subject first. With no card due it falls back to the sums.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[3. Mental Math \& Euler Dojo (Camera-Free)]{3. Mental Math \& Euler Dojo \texorpdfstring{\pill{Camera-Free}}{(Camera-Free)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{1 to 10 problems in a row} (default 3), positive answers of at most four digits. One wrong answer resets the run to zero and draws a new problem, so guessing digit by digit gets nowhere.
  \item \textbf{Euler mental-calculation mode} (on by default) widens the problems to 11 families: mental squares ($a^2 + c$), $2\times2$ determinants ($ab - cd$), cubes ($a^3 \pm b$), powers of two (up to $2^{13}$), modular products, Euler's totient $\varphi(n)$ for $n \le 100$, and the series $\sum k$ and $\sum k^2$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[4. Tomorrow's Top Three (19:40)]{4. Tomorrow's Top Three \texorpdfstring{\pill{19:40, Fixed}}{(19:40, Fixed)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A fixed alarm twenty minutes before bed that stops when you write up to three things that matter most tomorrow (one line is enough). Nothing judges the day.
  \item The lines show on Home and on the launcher the next day, and the newest evening syncs to your other devices. The row cannot be edited, but snooze and the emergency dismiss work as on an ordinary alarm.
\end{itemize}

% SECTION 3: NUCLEAR FOCUS
% =============================================================================
\needspace{10\baselineskip}
\section{Nuclear Focus, Session Summaries \& Sound}

\subsection{Unstoppable Sessions (\texttt{PomodoroTimerService})}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fixed commitments only:} \textbf{1\,h}, \textbf{1\,h\,30}, \textbf{2\,h}, or \textbf{3\,h} ($6 \times 25$ min focus, 5 min breaks, a 15 min long break after the third block $= 3$\,h\,05), or an \textbf{exact session for a calendar event} (25/5, last block trimmed to end on the event's minute). The event's title is kept beside the course in the history, synced and exported.
  \item \textbf{No pause, reset or stop} anywhere: app, notification, floating bubble or watch. Time is counted on \texttt{SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()}, so it never drifts.
  \item \textbf{Whole-session wall, breaks included:} every app outside your study list is walled on phone, tablet and watch, Android Settings too; study apps can be removed mid-session but never added; Priority Do Not Disturb turns on everywhere and is restored afterwards.
  \item \textbf{Survives reboots and force-stops} (\texttt{SessionRecovery}): an interrupted block resumes as \emph{withered}, an overdue one is logged \texttt{SKIPPED}.
  \item \textbf{Bed hours take no work:} no session starts (or would run) between 20:00 and 03:00 on any device.
  \item \textbf{Immersive timer:} during focus the screen shows only the ring, ``Break at HH:MM'', an optional Euler quote, and \textbf{Park a thought}; when a break ends the timer comes back to the front by itself.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Session Summary (Euler Derivation Protocol)}
When a session's last focus block ends, the ringing phone asks for \textbf{at least ten minutes} writing on paper everything studied --- (1) definitions and theorems, (2) the crux derivation or a counterexample, (3) the next open question --- and then a photo of the page.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Until the photo is kept (30 minutes at most), apps outside the study list stay walled and no new session can start on any device. The ten minutes are counted on time since boot, so moving the clock forward opens nothing.
  \item Photos stay on the phone (newest 90 days), are filed by course, can be retaken for ten minutes, and are counted per course per week on Home and in the weekly report. They are never uploaded.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Forest, Streak, Recall \& the Adversaria}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Focus Forest:} each verified block grows a tree; with Deep Focus on, 10\,s in a non-study app withers it (screen off never counts).
  \item \textbf{Focus streak:} a day counts when verified focus minutes reach the daily goal (up to 12\,h, synced). In the two weeks before an exam the goal rises by itself: $+25\%$, then $+50\%$, then double in the last three days.
  \item \textbf{Recall notes:} after each block, two lines of recall wait on the Flashcards screen to become cards.
  \item \textbf{Adversaria:} parked thoughts marked as questions form a ledger of open problems and conjectures; one can be pinned to the timer and focus screen.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Study Sounds}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Seven seamless loops} (rain, fireplace, waves, creek, warm pad, soft static, wind in the pines): 44.1\,kHz stereo OGG Vorbis, built circularly so there is no seam, levelled to equal loudness, with 0--60\,s fades.
  \item \textbf{Your own music}, played inside the app from a folder you pick or from your library (album or artist), shuffled. Calls and navigation pause it; anything unreadable falls back to a loop, never silence. No Spotify or other music app is opened or exempted from the walls.
\end{itemize}

\needspace{10\baselineskip}
\section{Study Suite: Flashcards, AI Help, Exams \& Reports}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{SM-2 flashcards} (\texttt{Sm2Scheduler}) by subject, with \emph{Again / Hard / Good / Easy}.
  \item \textbf{AI card drafting (optional, your own Gemini key):} from typed notes or a photo of a page, \texttt{gemini-3.8-flash} drafts four kinds of research card: proof skeleton and crux step, hypothesis \& counterexample audit, method trigger, and definition or formula.
  \item \textbf{Study chat (optional):} \emph{Explain / Quiz}, or \emph{Euler Referee (Socratic)}, which asks for definitions and hypotheses first, probes boundary cases and offers counterexamples to loose claims.
  \item \textbf{Exams:} countdowns on Home; for the seven days up to an exam every gated app is shut (an exam week, once held, cannot be shortened by deleting or moving the exam).
  \item \textbf{Honest screen time:} usage is folded from raw \texttt{UsageEvents} per activity, split cleanly at midnight; study apps and Euler's Lab itself are not counted as screen time. Study time from the tablet, paper or the watch is credited without double counting.
  \item \textbf{Course credits \& sleep score:} the weekly report weighs each course's hours against its credits and scores how many nights the screen stayed off between 20:00 and 03:00.
  \item \textbf{On-device weekly report:} wins, watch-outs, one suggestion for next week, time by course and by calendar event, and summary pages per course --- written on the device with nothing sent to any AI.
\end{itemize}

\needspace{10\baselineskip}
\section{Distraction Walls \& Schedule Rules}

\subsection{Always-On Gatekeeper \& Verdict Priority}
\texttt{AppBlockService} is a \texttt{specialUse} foreground service (never an \texttt{AccessibilityService}) that checks the apps on screen every \textbf{500\,ms} while the screen is on. One pure function, \texttt{gateVerdict}, decides every wall in this order:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}c L{3.6cm} Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\sffamily \#} & \textbf{\sffamily Verdict} & \textbf{\sffamily Rule \& Condition} \\
\midrule
\textbf{1} & \texttt{ADMIN\_LOCK\_WALL} & The device-admin off switch in Settings, while the optional uninstall lock is on. \\
\textbf{2} & \texttt{ALLOW} & \textbf{Essentials:} phone and in-call screen, home screen, Euler's Lab, permission controller, Samsung screen capture (Settings is walled during a session). A home or SMS app newly made the default is quarantined first. \\
\textbf{3} & \texttt{CLOCK\_WALL} & Outside a session, automatic time or zone off, or the clock more than 5 min from network time: everything but Clock, Calendar, the SMS app and the Wi-Fi sign-in page. \\
\textbf{4} & \texttt{SLEEP\_WALL} & \textbf{Night wall:} \textbf{18:00--08:00} on the phone, \textbf{20:00--03:00} on the tablet. Clock, Calendar and (on the phone) the cameras stay open. \\
\textbf{5} & \texttt{FOCUS\_WALL} & A session is running on any device and the app is not on the study list. \\
\textbf{6} & \texttt{SUMMARY\_WALL} & A session summary is owed (up to 30 min after the session). \\
\textbf{7} & \texttt{QUARANTINE\_WALL} / \texttt{HISTORY\_WALL} & A new app in its first 7 days (sideloaded build); or Notification History is on (it would keep the text the walls remove). \\
\textbf{8} & \texttt{CAP\_WALL} & A per-app daily limit is spent. \\
\textbf{9} & \texttt{EXAM\_WALL} & Gated apps during the 7 days up to an exam. \\
\textbf{10} & \texttt{WINDOW\_WALL} & Gated apps outside \textbf{12:25--12:50}, every day. \\
\textbf{11} & \texttt{BEDTIME\_WALL} & Gated apps from 16:00 to 08:20 (the daily window lies wholly outside it). \\
\textbf{12} & \texttt{ALLOW} & Everything else, and gated apps inside the window, with no countdown or budget. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Priority order of \texttt{gateVerdict} in \texttt{AppGate.kt}.}
\end{table}

\subsection{What Counts as Gated}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Social, video and portals:} Instagram, Threads, Facebook, X, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Tumblr, BeReal, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube (and Music, Kids, NewPipe, ReVanced), Netflix, Twitch, Samsung Free/TV Plus, Naver, Daum, the Google app's Discover and Search, and the Play Store --- plus every app in Android's social and video categories.
  \item \textbf{Every messenger:} WhatsApp (and Business), Telegram, KakaoTalk, Messenger, Signal, Discord, LINE, WeChat, Viber. The default SMS app is never gated outside a session, so bank codes always arrive.
  \item \textbf{Every browser, Chrome included}, and web-app shells. Coursework goes through the built-in Blackboard browser, locked to the university's and Blackboard's hosts.
  \item \textbf{Gemini stays open:} standalone Gemini runs inside the Google app on Android~16; \texttt{AppGate.effectivePackage} tells its activities apart from Discover and Search, so Gemini works while the feed stays walled.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Fixed Walls \& Guards}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fixed in code:} no setting can move or loosen a wall; a daily app limit can only be tightened.
  \item \textbf{Notifications and media:} notifications from walled apps are removed (by package, never by content) and their media sessions paused, so picture-in-picture or background audio cannot leak through.
  \item \textbf{Pop-ups, split screen and pinning} are covered; a walled app's background process is ended once it leaves the screen.
  \item \textbf{Night:} a lights-out notice at 20:00, and from 20:30 a reminder every 30 min if the phone is below $40\%$ and unplugged, so a flat battery cannot beat the 03:00 alarm.
  \item \textbf{Lost grants:} revoking Usage access or draw-over-apps drops every wall; an alert returns every 15 minutes until it is granted again.
  \item \textbf{Uninstall lock (optional):} a plain device admin with no policies, so the app cannot be uninstalled casually; there is no off button in the app. Euler's Lab is never a device owner.
\end{itemize}

\section{Minimal Home Screen \& Calendar Focus}
Making Euler's Lab the home app (\texttt{LauncherActivity}) replaces badges and app grids with a quiet dark text screen:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fixed tool pins:} Phone, Camera, Expert RAW, Samsung Notes, Claude, Gemini, Outlook, KakaoBank, UNIST Smart-Check, Clock, Calendar, and the Blackboard browser. Gated apps can never be pinned.
  \item \textbf{Today's agenda:} every event today; tick items off, get a 5-minute heads-up, or tap an event to start a session timed to end on its last minute. Tomorrow's top three shows here too.
  \item \textbf{``Other apps'':} no search bar, no index; the grey list reshuffles every \textbf{4 seconds}. Capped apps get a \textbf{45-second pause}; walled apps open a screen saying when they next open (\emph{``today at 12:25''}). If the wall service is down, the launcher applies the same rules itself.
\end{itemize}

% =============================================================================
\needspace{10\baselineskip}
\section{Wear OS, Cloud Sync \& Euler's Lab Clip}

\subsection{Wear OS Companion (\texttt{:wear})}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Own Nuclear timer:} 1\,h to 3\,h sessions on the wrist with no phone nearby, the same no-pause rule, strong phase-change buzzes and a dim ambient countdown. It can follow a phone session (\emph{Continue here}) and refuses starts during bed hours or while a summary is owed.
  \item \textbf{Face, tiles and complication:} a Compose pager (focus ring with the event title, today, progress), the \emph{Euler's Lab progress} tile, the \emph{Euler's Lab Today} agenda tile, a watch-face complication, and an ongoing-activity indicator.
  \item \textbf{Watch walls:} gated apps on the watch open only 12:25--12:50 (tighten-only, following the phone's window), shut during sessions, summaries and exam weeks; their notifications are removed. Apps are never disabled.
  \item \textbf{Sync:} finished blocks go to the phone over the Data Layer (with a 14-day acknowledgement list) and, when signed in, straight to Firestore over Wi-Fi or LTE.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cloud Sync \& Backups}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Opt-in Firestore mirror:} Google sign-in (Credential Manager). Every row carries \texttt{syncId}, \texttt{updatedAt}, \texttt{deleted} (tombstone) and \texttt{pending}; device time decides last-write-wins, server time drives the pull cursor. Access rules keep every record to its own account.
  \item \textbf{Backups:} CSV export of the history (with course and event columns) and a full JSON backup that merges without destroying anything and re-arms alarms. Summary photos are never in the backup.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Euler's Lab Clip --- Encrypted Clipboard}
A shared clipboard for text and screenshots across phone, tablet and the Ubuntu laptop:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{AES-256-GCM} with a 32-byte key exchanged only by QR code over the local network, kept in Android's encrypted storage and the GNOME keyring.
  \item \textbf{LAN first} (mDNS, port 47321); when the network isolates devices, \textbf{chunked encrypted Firestore documents} that expire after an hour, bounded in size by the security rules. Clips marked sensitive by a password manager are never sent.
\end{itemize}

\section{Privacy, Permissions \& Architecture}

\begin{wpbox}[wpteal]{No Ads $\cdot$ No Analytics $\cdot$ Offline by Default}
Euler's Lab has \textbf{no advertising, no analytics and no developer server}. Without Cloud Sync sign-in or your own Gemini key it makes \textbf{no internet requests}, and every alarm, focus session, wall, flashcard and report works offline. App data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfers.
\end{wpbox}
